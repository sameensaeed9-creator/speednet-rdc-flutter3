SpeedNet Client QR Flow

New flow:
1. Client buys package and submits payment proof.
2. Admin logs in and approves payment.
3. System requests real SpeedNet QR/code.
4. Client opens my-subscriptions.html (My eSIMs).
5. Client enters phone number or order ID.
6. Approved SpeedNet eSIM QR/code appears for client.
7. Client can copy code or download QR image.
